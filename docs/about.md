# About

<span class="uk-eyebrow">DevOps · Platform · AI automation</span>

I'm **Umesh Khatiwada**, a DevOps, platform and AI automation engineer based in Kathmandu, Nepal, working with teams worldwide. I've spent more than four years building Kubernetes infrastructure, GitOps pipelines, Go microservices and AI agents that watch production.

This blog is where I write up what worked (and what broke) along the way. The guides are practical: commands you can run, manifests you can apply, and notes on the gotchas.

## What you'll find here

- **Observability:** Prometheus, Grafana, Alertmanager integrations and log pipelines.
- **Security:** cluster scanning, image signing and admission policies.
- **Platform:** autoscaling, ingress, multi-cluster orchestration, backup and restore.
- **Agentic AI:** agents and the Model Context Protocol applied to operations.
